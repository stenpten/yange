# Third-party notices

## Cliopatria

Political borders in `public/history/polities` are simplified from Cliopatria v0.2.1 by the Seshat Global History Databank.

https://github.com/Seshat-Global-History-Databank/cliopatria

https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-025-04516-9

Cliopatria is CC BY 4.0. Those files stay under CC BY 4.0. They are not relicensed as GPL. The project simplified the geometry, dropped relation overlays, split Taiwan out of foreign polygons, and removed a misdated Great Yuan slice.

## historical-basemaps

Earlier editions of this map used simplified historical-basemaps by Andre Ourednik, GPL-3.0. Those snapshot files are no longer shipped. The live political layer is Cliopatria.

https://github.com/aourednik/historical-basemaps

## Natural Earth

`public/history/land.geojson` is simplified from Natural Earth 1:110m land.

https://www.naturalearthdata.com/

Natural Earth vector data is in the public domain.

## Mapterhorn

Terrain shading uses Mapterhorn Terrarium elevation tiles at zoom 0-3. The tiles are requested at runtime and are not redistributed in this repository.

https://mapterhorn.com/attribution
