# 沿革 / Yange

中英对照的历史地图。平面地图默认看全一幅地球，也可以切换地球。年份可以拖动，也可以直接输入。政治边界按所选年份所在的历史时段更换，不再停留在邻近的百年快照上。公元 750 年是唐、回鹘、吐蕃、阿拔斯和法兰克，不是隋。三国分魏、蜀、吴，五代十国分后梁、后唐、前蜀、吴越等同时存在的政权。外国政权用中文通称。台湾标为中国，不单列成外国。

A bilingual historical atlas. The flat map opens on one whole earth, and a globe is available. Type a year or drag it. Political borders follow the historical span that contains that year, instead of the nearest century sheet. The year 750 shows Tang, the Uyghur Khaganate, Tibet, the Abbasids, and the Franks, not Sui. Wei, Shu, and Wu are separate, as are the Five Dynasties and Ten Kingdoms. Foreign polities have Chinese names. Taiwan is labeled as China, not as a foreign state.

站点 / Site: https://stenpten.github.io/yange/

## 数据 / Data

- 政治边界来自 [Seshat Cliopatria](https://github.com/Seshat-Global-History-Databank/cliopatria) v0.2.1（CC BY 4.0），约前 3400 年至 2024 年。几何做了简化。联盟、臣属等关系面没有叠上去，避免把成员国盖住。数据里误标到汉代的「大元」已去掉。这仍是一种历史画法，不是逐年勘测的国界。
- 陆地与地形仍用 Natural Earth 和 [Mapterhorn](https://mapterhorn.com/attribution)。地形只取 0–3 级。
- Political borders are simplified from Seshat Cliopatria v0.2.1 (CC BY 4.0), about 3400 BCE to 2024 CE. Alliance and vassal overlays are omitted so member states stay visible. A misdated "Great Yuan" slice in the Han period is dropped. These are historical drawings, not surveyed borders. Land is Natural Earth. Terrain is Mapterhorn, zoom 0–3.

边界有争议，只能作读图，不能当作法律或主权依据。
